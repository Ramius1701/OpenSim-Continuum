Land auctions

Browse the grid's configured auction regions. Bids reserve the full amount; outbid reservations are released. Auctions transfer land, not parcel objects.

To bid or manage an auction, log in here and use your viewer to visit that region. Your live viewer session authorizes the reservation. After a lost reply, reload before retrying the same bid.
